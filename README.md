# Gikomba Market Stall Performance Analysis

**Author:** Anne Mugunye · PLP Data Analytics 1 Million Devs Cohort

## Business problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For the last two weeks she has written her daily sales in a notebook and kept a rough record of her stock. She wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, which products she should restock, and which days she should focus on. This project answers those questions with core Python only (lists, dictionaries, loops and functions, with no pandas or NumPy) and ends with recommendations backed by numbers.

## How to run

Open the notebook in Google Colab and select **Runtime → Run all**: [![Open In Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/assets/colab-badge.svg)](https://colab.research.google.com/github/annykaym6-cpu/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top three findings

1. **The business is growing.** Sales rose from KES 22,050 in week 1 to KES 24,400 in week 2, which is 10.7% week-on-week growth (14-day total: KES 46,450).
2. **Weekends are the strongest days.** Saturday and Sunday average KES 4,900 against KES 2,685 on weekdays, which is 82.5% more, and all 4 "Strong" days were weekends.
3. **Two products need restocking now.** Tomatoes (18 kg against a reorder level of 25 kg) and Sukuma (9 kg against 15 kg) are below their reorder levels.
